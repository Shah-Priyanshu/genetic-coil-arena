# Cheap probes of three directions (2026-10-29)

Exploratory probes, not goal tests. Each took minutes of CPU. They are meant to show where pre-registered work is worth doing.

| Direction | Probe | Result | Signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Learned coordination in a drone swarm | Several gas sources (`r14_multi_probe.md`) | 3 sources: tuned hand design finds all in 61.9%; perfect sharing of what the swarm smells, 93.1% (+31 points, 77 steps faster). 1 source: only +2 points. 2 B = 8 B throughout | **Promising, untested.** There is room, and it lies in coordination (who goes where, when to move on), not in compression. A learned policy has not been trained yet |
| 2. Separately trained parts joined after the fact | Bridge between squad dialects (`r7e_interlingua.md`) | Raw: 0% of native. Bridged through the G12 meaning space: 71% ± 8. The reader is near perfect (the true-status ceiling is 73%) | **Works, capped by the vocabulary.** A richer shared meaning space is the lever |
| 3. Real-data specialists (the product path) | k-shot adapter on RE1 (`r13b_kshot.md`) | Online Boutique: 52.8% → 83.3% joint with 20 labelled incidents (BARO 54.4%); SS 55 → 63; TT 32 → 50. A model from scratch on the same labels does as well from k = 10 | **Practical.** About 20 labels per new system fix transfer. It holds when splitting by repetition and for services never labelled (Online Boutique 54 → 78.5 at k = 20). Feature design carries over; pretrained weights matter little |

2. **Direction 1, learned coordination** (CPU, about a day): train swarm policies with ES on the 3-source task against the tuned hand design and the sensed-oracle bound. Pre-register: pass if the learned swarm beats the hand design on all-found by ≥ 10 points.
3. **Direction 2, richer meaning space** (CPU, hours): add food distance bins or a learned vocabulary to the G12 writer and see whether the bridge ceiling rises above 73%.
